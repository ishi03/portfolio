Research and HCI

Interfaces are part of the intelligence.

My HCI lens is practical: AI tools should be evaluated by whether people can understand, correct, trust, and use them in context.

Human-centered AI is a systems problem.

I am interested in AI-human interaction, adaptive interfaces, user behavior and feedback loops, calm computing, peripheral attention, and evaluation methods that look beyond raw model accuracy.

Adaptive interfaces Designing systems that change in response to user context while staying predictable and legible.
Feedback loops Capturing user signals in ways that improve model behavior without overburdening the user.
Evaluation beyond accuracy Looking at usefulness, confidence calibration, error recovery, speed, cognitive load, and trust.
Production constraints Bringing auditability, reliability, safety guardrails, observability, and release discipline into intelligent product work.
